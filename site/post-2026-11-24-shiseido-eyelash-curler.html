How to get the most out of it

  • Curl first, mascara second. Both Who What Wear and Reviewed curl bare lashes and then apply mascara, which keeps the curler clean and locks in the shape. Pair it with a lifting formula like Maybelline Sky High for extra length.
  • Walk it up the lashes. Shiseido's directions (shared on Sephora) say to start at the base, squeeze gently, then move partway up and squeeze again, finishing near the tips. Reviewed suggests about three squeezes for a natural, rounded curl.
  • Keep the pressure light. Who What Wear and BestReviews both note that a gentle squeeze is all it takes; pressing hard is what leads to pinching.
  • Wipe it after every use. Shiseido recommends wiping the curler and pads with a tissue between uses so mascara residue doesn't build up.
  • Swap the pad when it looks worn. Use the included refill once the original pad loses its bounce; Reviewed's general advice is to replace pads every two to three months.
