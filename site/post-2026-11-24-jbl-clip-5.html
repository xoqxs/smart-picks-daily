Why people love it

Big sound from a tiny body

This is the headline. Tom's Guide described bass that's well defined with clear vocals on top, TechRadar summed it up as "big bass in a little case," and NBC Select found it easily filled a bedroom-sized space. For a speaker this compact, it brings real energy to a playlist on the patio or at a picnic.

A clip that actually goes everywhere

JBL widened the carabiner opening on this generation, and reviewers put it to work right away. Tom's Guide hooked it to belt loops, backpacks, handlebars and even a shower head; NBC Select clipped it to towel rods and bag handles; Pack Hacker kept it on a pack strap while cycling. Pick a spot and the music comes with you.

Ready for water, sand and steam

With an official IP67 rating, the Clip 5 is built to shrug off dust and water. Tom's Guide and TechRadar recommend it for beach days, and TechRadar and NBC Select both had it handle the shower without trouble. That makes it equally at home by the pool, in the bathroom or out in a light rain.

All-day playtime

JBL rates it at up to 12 hours, and testers landed close to that at moderate volume: Tom's Guide and TechRadar each recorded around 11 hours, while Pack Hacker reached the full 12 at half volume or lower. It's enough to cover a long hike or a full day at the lake on one charge.

More speakers, more sound

Tap the Auracast button and the Clip 5 can join other Auracast-enabled JBL speakers, or team up with a second Clip 5 for true stereo. TechRadar paired it with a Go 4 and an Xtreme 4, and Tom's Guide and SoundGuys both highlight the stereo option. If you already own newer JBL gear, this turns a personal speaker into part of a bigger setup.

How to get the most out of it

  • Download the JBL Portable app. Tom's Guide, TechRadar and SoundGuys all point to it for EQ presets, a custom equalizer, stereo pairing and firmware updates. The speaker works fine without it, but the custom EQ is the easiest way to fine-tune the sound.
  • Save Playtime Boost for long outings. It squeezes out extra hours, but Tom's Guide, SoundGuys, NBC Select and Pack Hacker all noticed the sound gets thinner with it on, so keep it off when audio quality matters most.
  • Keep the volume sensible. TechRadar and SoundGuys found the treble can turn sharp near maximum, and Pack Hacker got the best battery life at half volume or below. A step or two down from max sounds cleaner and lasts longer.
  • Hang it, don't bury it. Reviewers at TechRadar and Pack Hacker got the most from it clipped to a door handle, shelf or pack strap rather than tucked inside a bag, so the sound isn't muffled.
  • Grab a second one for stereo. Two Clip 5s paired through Auracast give you left and right channels, which SoundGuys and Tom's Guide note is the way to get a wider soundstage.

FAQ

Is the JBL Clip 5 waterproof?

Yes. JBL rates the Clip 5 IP67, meaning it's waterproof and dustproof, which is why reviewers recommend it for the beach, the pool and the shower.

How long does the JBL Clip 5 battery last?

JBL quotes up to 12 hours per charge, with up to 3 more hours using Playtime Boost. Reviewers measured roughly 11 to 12 hours at moderate volume, and it takes about 3 hours to recharge.

Can you pair two JBL Clip 5 speakers?

Yes. Using Auracast, two Clip 5s can pair for stereo sound, and the Clip 5 can also link with other Auracast-enabled JBL speakers.

What's the difference between the JBL Clip 5 and Clip 4?

The Clip 5 adds Auracast multi-speaker support, the JBL Portable app with EQ controls and a wider carabiner, and reviewers found its sound fuller and bassier.
