Why people love it

Blend, drink and go from one cup

This is the Magic Bullet's signature trick. You blend right in the cup, swap the blade for a lid, and walk out the door. Homes & Gardens and Best Buy owners both point out how handy the extra lids are for stashing leftovers or carrying a drink to work, and the flip-top to-go lid makes it a natural for busy mornings.

So simple anyone can use it

There are no settings to figure out. Press the cup down to pulse, or lock it in place and let it run on its own. CNN Underscored and Homes & Gardens both praise how quickly it clicks for first-timers, and Reviewed likes that you don't have to keep holding a button while it runs.

Smooth single-serve smoothies and shakes

For the job it's designed for, it shines. Reviewed and Homes & Gardens both got smooth fruit-and-greens smoothies out of it, Food Network found fresh-fruit blends came together in about half a minute, and CNN Underscored liked it for quick protein shakes because the small cups leave less powder stuck to the sides.

A generous set in one box

Three cups, two lip rings, a to-go lid and two storage lids is a lot of kit in one box. Food Network, CNN Underscored and Homes & Gardens all call out the extras as a big part of the value, and they make it easy to blend for two people back to back.

Tiny footprint, easy cleanup

The base is about the width of a coffee mug, so it tucks into a cabinet or deep drawer between uses. Cleanup is just as painless: Food Network, Reviewed and the brand all note that the cups and lids go on the dishwasher's top rack, and the smooth cylindrical cups have few corners for food to hide in.

How to get the most out of it

  • Add enough liquid. Milk, juice or water helps everything move. Reviewed and longtime owners say this matters most with frozen fruit.
  • Skip straight ice cubes. Homes & Gardens, Reviewed and Best Buy owners agree plain ice is beyond this motor. Use frozen fruit for a cold, thick drink instead.
  • Blend in short bursts. For thick dips or nuts, run it for a bit, stop, give the cup a shake, and go again. Homes & Gardens and owners say this keeps the motor from working too hard.
  • Give frozen ingredients a little extra time. Food Network and Best Buy reviewers found frozen fruit blends smoothly, it just takes a few more seconds than fresh.
  • Keep to single servings. The cups are sized for one drink at a time. Food Network and CNN Underscored both found it works best when you don't overfill.

FAQ

Can the Magic Bullet crush ice?

Not well. Reviewers found plain ice cubes rattle around rather than break down. It handles frozen fruit in smoothies when you add enough liquid.

What comes in the 11-piece Magic Bullet set?

A 250-watt motor base, a stainless steel cross blade, a tall cup, a short cup, a handled party mug, two lip rings, a flip-top to-go lid, two resealable lids and a recipe guide.

Is the Magic Bullet dishwasher safe?

The cups and lids can go on the top rack of the dishwasher. Magic Bullet says to keep the motor base out of water and wipe it with a damp cloth instead.

What can I make with the Magic Bullet?

Magic Bullet lists smoothies, shakes, dips, sauces, salsas and desserts, plus chopping and grating small amounts of food. It's designed for single servings.
