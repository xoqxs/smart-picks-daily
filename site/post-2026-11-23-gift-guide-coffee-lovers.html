Smart ways to give a coffee gift

  • Match the brewer to the household. The AeroPress and Hario V60 shine for one or two cups, while the Chemex is the pick for sharing a pot.
  • Build a pour-over bundle. A Hario V60 or Chemex plus the COSORI kettle and the Etekcity scale is a complete starter kit; add filters and a bag of their favorite beans.
  • Tuck in the right filters. The V60 uses size 02 cone filters and the Chemex works with Chemex Bonded filters, so a pack in the box saves a trip to the store.
  • Add a care note. The Zojirushi mug should be washed by hand, while the YETI Rambler and the AeroPress can go in the dishwasher.
