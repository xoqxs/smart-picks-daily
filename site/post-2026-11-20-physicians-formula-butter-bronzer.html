How to get the most out of it

  • Think "3" or "E" shape. Sweep it from the temples down along the cheekbones and under the jaw, the areas the sun naturally hits. Beauty Bay suggests circular motions toward the temples, then blending.
  • Tap off the excess. The formula is creamy and pigmented, so tap your brush before applying and build up slowly instead of going heavy in one pass.
  • Try your own brush. The included sponge is handy on the go, but ChickAdvisor reviewers note a fluffy powder brush gives a smoother, more diffused result at home.
  • Add a touch to the nose. A light sweep across the bridge of the nose sells the sun-kissed effect, a tip Beauty Bay shares as well.
  • Layer it over cream products. It sits nicely over cream or liquid foundation, so you can set your base and add warmth in a single step.
