How to get the most out of it

  • Start with damp, not dripping, hair. Color Wow and PureWow both advise towel-drying first; the brand says the product can't activate properly on dry hair.
  • Be generous and work in sections. The brand recommends spraying each section liberally, and PureWow's reviewer saw the best results with several pumps per side rather than a light spritz.
  • Bring the heat and the tension. Dream Coat needs a blow-dryer to work, as Color Wow, Marie Claire and Cool Mom Picks all stress. Dry each section completely with a round or paddle brush for the sleekest finish.
  • Skip heavy products underneath. Color Wow advises against layering oils or rich conditioners at the damp stage so the coating can bond evenly.
  • Clarify now and then. If hair starts to feel coated, a clarifying shampoo helps, a tip shared by both Color Wow and Cool Mom Picks.
