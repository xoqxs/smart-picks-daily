How to get the most out of it

  • Start below the max line. StyleCaster found that a full two cups could bubble up under the lid, while a bit less cooked neatly. Begin with a slightly smaller batch and adjust from there.
  • Follow the recipe guide's ratios. Different grains need different amounts of water. Scripps News and The Daily Beast both recommend the chart in Dash's booklet as your starting point.
  • Watch the lever, not your ears. There's no beep. When the switch pops from Cook to Warm, your food is ready, so set a phone timer for around 20 minutes if you plan to step away.
  • Need more time? Press it again. For heartier dishes, The Daily Beast notes you can push the lever back down to keep cooking once it switches to Warm.
  • Treat the coating gently. Use the included paddle or a wooden spoon instead of metal utensils so the nonstick surface keeps releasing food cleanly.
