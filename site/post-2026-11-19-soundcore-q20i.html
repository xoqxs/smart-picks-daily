Why people love it

Real quiet on planes, trains and buses

The Q20i uses two microphones inside each ear cup and two outside to listen for noise and cancel it. It shines on steady, low-pitched sounds like jet engines, bus motors, air conditioners and fans. TechGearLab measured roughly 25 dB of reduction, OuterAudio calls the cancelling a big step up for the money, and owners on the soundcore subreddit describe it as plenty for the gym and travel.

A battery that outlasts your week

Anker rates it at 40 hours with ANC on and 60 hours with it off, and independent testing went even further. In practice, that means a week of commuting or a long-haul round trip on one charge. Forgot to charge? Five minutes on USB-C buys about four hours of listening, which is perfect while you pack.

Big, fun sound you can tune

Large 40 mm drivers and Anker's BassUp give music a punchy, energetic low end that suits pop, hip-hop and workout playlists. Comfortable for long sessions

Smart everyday features

Multipoint lets you stay connected to your laptop and phone at the same time, so a call can interrupt your video without re-pairing. Transparency mode lets announcements and voices through when you need to hear the world, and the app adds a white-noise option for focus or naps.

How to get the most out of it

  • Open the app on day one. Install the soundcore app, update the firmware and try a few EQ presets. If the bass feels like too much, a balanced preset or turning BassUp down makes a big difference.
  • Pick the right mode. Use ANC for planes and open offices, Transparency for walking in traffic or catching announcements, and Normal mode when you want the longest battery life.
  • Keep the AUX cable in your bag. If the battery ever runs flat, plug in the included cable and keep listening; just note that ANC switches off in wired mode.
  • Top up while you get ready. A five-minute USB-C charge gives roughly four hours, and a full charge takes about two.
  • Make sure it's the "i". The Q20i is the USB-C model with app support; the older Life Q20 uses micro-USB and lacks Transparency mode.

FAQ

How long does the soundcore Q20i battery last?

Anker rates it at up to 40 hours with noise cancelling on and up to 60 hours with it off. A five-minute charge adds about four hours.

Can it connect to two devices at once?

Yes. Bluetooth multipoint lets it stay paired with two devices, such as a phone and a laptop, at the same time.

What's the difference between the Q20i and the Life Q20?

The Q20i adds USB-C charging, a Transparency mode and soundcore app support with custom EQ. The older Life Q20 uses micro-USB and has no app control.

Can I use it with a cable?

Yes. It comes with an AUX cable for wired listening, including Hi-Res audio. Noise cancelling is off in wired mode.
