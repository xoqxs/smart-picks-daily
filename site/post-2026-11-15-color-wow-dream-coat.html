Who it's for

People who blow-dry or straighten their hair and want it to stay smooth in rain, steam or summer humidity. It's a great fit before a trip somewhere muggy. If you have curls and want to keep them, COLOR WOW itself recommends its separate Dream Coat for Curly Hair instead of this original version.

How it works differently

Heat switches it on. The brand's FAQ says the formula is heat activated, so spraying it and air-drying won't give you the full effect. Blow-drying with a brush and some tension is what creates that glassy finish.

It builds a coating, not a mask. Dream Coat works on the surface of the hair, which is why COLOR WOW says it doesn't interfere with keratin or Brazilian blowout treatments.

One application, several days. Because the results are designed to last through three to four washes, you don't need to reapply it every single morning.

Biggest mistake to avoid

Don't spray it on dry hair and expect a change. Apply it to towel-dried, damp hair, comb it through, then dry in sections. If you prefer air-drying, you'll still get some benefit by running a flat iron over dry hair afterwards, according to the brand.

Quick checklist

  • Do you blow-dry? Then this is worth trying.
  • Curly and want to keep curls? Choose the curly version.
  • Apply on damp hair, then use heat.
