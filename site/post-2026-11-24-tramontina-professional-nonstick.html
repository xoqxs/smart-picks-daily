How to get the most out of it

  • Keep the heat low to medium. TODAY, CNN Underscored and Food Network all recommend gentle heat for nonstick pans. Preheat briefly with a little oil or butter rather than blasting an empty pan.
  • Skip the cooking spray. CNN Underscored, Food Network and the AOL-published guide all warn that sprays can leave a sticky film. A small pat of butter or a teaspoon of oil works better.
  • Use wood or silicone utensils. Metal spatulas can scratch any nonstick coating, a point CNN Underscored and TODAY both make.
  • Hand-wash when you can. The pan is dishwasher-safe, but TODAY, CNN Underscored and Food Network all suggest a soft sponge and warm, soapy water to keep the surface slick longer. Give the area around the rivets a quick extra wipe, since TODAY and CNN Underscored both note food can collect there.
  • Slide off the grip for the oven or a deep clean. Remove the silicone sleeve before baking and wash it separately, as CNN Underscored suggests.
