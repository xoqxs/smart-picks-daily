How to get the most out of it

  • Load everything first. You can't add ingredients while the blades spin, so put your liquid, fruit and ice in the cup before you lock it on, and don't fill past the cup's marked lines.
  • Blend in short bursts. Blender.com found shorter presses over a little more time gave better texture than one long hold. Pause, check the consistency, then press again.
  • Cut big frozen pieces down. Large frozen chunks can get stuck above the blades. Smaller pieces of frozen fruit, plus a splash of liquid, keep everything moving.
  • Keep it cold. The cups are meant for cold and room-temperature ingredients, so let cooked fruit or purees cool completely before blending.
  • Treat the parts gently. Real Homes puts the cup, lid and blade assembly on the dishwasher's top rack; if you notice lids fitting less snugly over time, a quick hand wash with warm soapy water is an easy alternative.

FAQ

Can the Ninja Fit crush ice and frozen fruit?

Yes. Ninja designs it to crush ice and frozen fruit, and reviewers found it handled both well. Cutting large frozen pieces smaller and adding a little liquid helps everything blend evenly.

Does the Ninja Fit have buttons or speed settings?

No. It has one speed and no buttons. Pressing the cup down onto the base runs the motor, and releasing it stops, so you control each burst yourself.

Are the Ninja Fit cups dishwasher safe?

Yes. The cups, lids and blade assembly are dishwasher safe, and the base can be wiped clean with a damp cloth.

Can you blend hot liquids in the Ninja Fit?

No. The Ninja Fit is meant for cold ingredients, so let anything cooked cool completely first, or use a full-size blender for hot soups.
