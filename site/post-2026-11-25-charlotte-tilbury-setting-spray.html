How to get the most out of it

  • Shake before every use. Charlotte Tilbury and Ulta both say to shake the bottle first. Woman & Home's makeup artist adds that shaking keeps the nozzle from clogging, so the mist stays fine.
  • Hold it 6–8 inches away, eyes closed. The brand's instructions and MissLJ Beauty both suggest this distance. Spraying in an X and then a T shape covers your whole face evenly.
  • Go light and build. The brand and Woman & Home both recommend thin layers instead of one heavy blast, which can disturb makeup you just applied.
  • Try the powder sandwich. Apply powder, mist, then press on a little more powder. Charlotte Tilbury says this gives a skin-like finish, and it pairs well with a loose powder like the one listed below.
  • Boost your eyeshadow. Mist a brush (or a cotton pad, per Woman & Home) before picking up shimmer or highlighter for more intense color and a wet-look shine.
