How to get the most out of it

  • Apply to clean, dry skin. Use it right after cleansing and before serums and moisturizer.
  • Skip the cotton pad if you like. Space NK's testers patted it on with their hands so less product soaked into cotton; a pad works too if you prefer a swipe.
  • Start slow, then build. Begin every second or third night and increase as your skin gets used to it; Grazia's and Marie Claire's writers both settled on every other night.
  • Follow with moisturizer. A good moisturizer keeps skin comfortable, especially in winter.
  • Wear sunscreen in the daytime. Paula's Choice recommends a broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher every morning when you exfoliate, and reviewers often keep BHA for nighttime.
  • Patch test first. Try it on a small area for a few days before going all over.

FAQ

How often should I use Paula's Choice 2% BHA?

The brand says once or twice a day, but most reviewers start every second or third night and build up. Listen to your skin and scale back if it feels tight or irritated.

Do I need to rinse it off?

No. It's a leave-on exfoliant. Apply it after cleansing, let it absorb, then continue with the rest of your routine.

Can I use it in the morning?

Yes, as long as you follow with a broad-spectrum sunscreen of SPF 30 or higher, as Paula's Choice advises. Many people simply keep it for their evening routine.

Is it fragrance-free?

Yes. The formula is fragrance-free and includes green tea, and reviewers note it has no noticeable scent.
