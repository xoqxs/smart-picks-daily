How to get the most out of it

  • Hand wash and dry right away. It keeps the colored coating looking new and prevents water spots.
  • Always use the guards. Slide each knife into its guard before it goes in the drawer to protect both the edge and your fingers.
  • Hone often, sharpen occasionally. A few strokes on a honing rod every few uses, plus a pull-through sharpener a few times a year, keeps them cutting cleanly.
  • Pick a color system. For example, red for raw meat, green for produce and yellow for bread, so everyone in the house uses the same knife for the same job.
  • Store them out of kids' reach. The bright colors look a lot like toys, so a high drawer is smart in a family kitchen.
