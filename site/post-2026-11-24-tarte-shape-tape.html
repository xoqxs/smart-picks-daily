How to get the most out of it

  • Start small. Reviewed sums it up neatly: "A little bit goes a long way." PureWow also recommends small dabs rather than one heavy swipe. Build up only where you need more coverage.
  • Blend in sections. Reviewed notes the formula sets quickly, so work on one under-eye at a time. Marie Claire likes letting it sit for a minute or two before blending blemish spots, which helps it grip and cover.
  • Use a damp sponge or a brush. Who What Wear and Marie Claire both reached for a damp beauty sponge, and Reviewed and PureWow suggest a brush for a smooth finish. The Real Techniques Miracle Complexion Sponge is a great match.
  • Go lighter under the eyes. tarte suggests picking a shade one to two shades lighter than your complexion for under-eye brightening, and keeping your true match for spots and redness.
  • Set it lightly. tarte recommends a light dusting of powder after blending. A fine loose powder such as the Laura Mercier Translucent Loose Setting Powder locks it in without adding heaviness.

FAQ

Is tarte Shape Tape good for under eyes?

Yes. It's one of the most popular uses for it. Applying a shade slightly lighter than your skin under the eyes and blending with a damp sponge or brush brightens dark circles, and tarte rates the formula for 12 hours of crease-proof wear.

Does Shape Tape concealer crease?

tarte describes it as crease-proof for up to 12 hours. A thin layer set with a light dusting of powder helps keep it smooth.

How do I choose my Shape Tape shade?

Match your skin for covering spots and redness, and go one to two shades lighter for under-eye brightening. The letter after each number shows the undertone, so 22N, for example, is a light shade with neutral undertones.

What's the difference between Shape Tape and Shape Tape Radiant?

The original Shape Tape offers full coverage with a natural matte finish, while Shape Tape Radiant gives medium coverage with a glowier, more skin-like finish.
