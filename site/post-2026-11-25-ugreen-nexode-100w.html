How to get the most out of it

  • Give your laptop a top port. Only USB-C1 and USB-C2 can reach 100W. iDownloadBlog recommends putting your most power-hungry device in the first USB-C port, and MacRumors notes the ports aren't labeled with their wattage, so it's worth memorizing which is which.
  • Know the two-laptop split. With laptops on C1 and C2, the charger splits power 65W and 30W. Put the bigger machine on C1.
  • Use C2 for fast phone charging when the bottom ports are busy. When USB-C3 and USB-A are both in use, they share a small 5V allowance (roughly 10.5W combined). That's fine for earbuds or a smartwatch, but a phone you want to top up quickly does better on a top port.
  • Expect slower charging as you add devices. As MacRumors puts it, "the more devices you're charging, the longer they'll all take to reach full battery capacity." With all four ports going, the main port drops to 45W.
  • Pair it with the right cable. Retailer B&H lists no cable in the box, so use a USB-C cable rated for 100W if you want full laptop speed.

FAQ

Can the UGREEN Nexode 100W charge a MacBook Pro?

Yes. USB-C1 and USB-C2 can each deliver up to 100W when used alone, and MacRumors and iDownloadBlog both reported fast MacBook Pro charging with the laptop on its own port. Charging slows when other devices share the charger.

Is 100W enough for two laptops?

UGREEN says it has enough power to charge two MacBooks at once. With laptops on C1 and C2, power splits 65W and 30W, so the second laptop charges more slowly than it would on its own.

Which port should I use for my laptop?

Use USB-C1 or USB-C2, the two ports that can reach 100W. USB-C3 and the USB-A port top out at 22.5W and are better for phones, earbuds and other small devices.

Does the UGREEN Nexode 100W have a foldable plug?

Yes, the US version's prongs fold into the body, which reviewers singled out as a travel-friendly touch.
