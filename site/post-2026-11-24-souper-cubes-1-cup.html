How to get the most out of it

  • Stop at the fill line. The Manual and Food Network both advise filling only to the marked line, which keeps portions even and the lid sitting flat. Weighing ingredients on a digital kitchen scale makes it easy to double a recipe for the freezer.
  • Give it time to freeze. Souper Cubes notes most foods take up to 24 hours to freeze fully, and watery foods like broth can take longer. Plan to unmold the next day.
  • Bag the blocks and reuse the tray. Move solid cubes into freezer bags so the tray is free for the next batch, a routine recommended by Souper Cubes, America's Test Kitchen and Homes & Gardens.
  • Release with a short rest. Let the tray sit out for 3 to 5 minutes before pushing on the bottom, as the brand and Food Network suggest.
  • Reheat your way. Drop a block into a pot on the stove, or thaw it overnight in the fridge and warm it in a microwave-safe bowl. Homes & Gardens even adds frozen soup concentrate straight to simmering stock.
