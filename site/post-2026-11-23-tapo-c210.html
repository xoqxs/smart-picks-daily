Does the Tapo C210 need a subscription?

No. It records to a microSD card for free, and live view works in the Tapo app. Tapo Care is an optional cloud plan that adds video history online.

Does the Tapo C210 work with 5GHz Wi-Fi?

No. TP-Link lists 2.4GHz Wi-Fi only, so make sure your router has a 2.4GHz network turned on during setup.

Can the Tapo C210 be used outdoors?

It's designed for indoor use and has no weatherproofing. For an outdoor-capable Tapo, consider the C120.
