Tech gift-giving tips

  • Check their phone first. AirTags work with iPhone, and the Find My app is where they show up. For an Android user, pick one of the headphones, the speaker or the power bank instead.
  • Match noise cancelling to their day. The soundcore Q30 and P30i block out engine and train noise, while the JBL Tune 520BT skips ANC in favor of a lighter build and a longer battery.
  • Bundle small with useful. A JBL Clip 5 with the Anker power bank makes a great camping or road-trip set, and the Samsung T7 pairs well with a new laptop.
  • Order early. Popular colors can sell out during Black Friday Week, so lock in the gift before the holiday rush.
