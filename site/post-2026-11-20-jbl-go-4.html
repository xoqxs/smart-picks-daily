Built for water, sand and drops

The IP67 rating means the speaker is sealed against dust and can handle a dunk in shallow water. TechRadar submerged it, What Hi-Fi reports it coped with drops and rain, and Tom's Guide suggests it for the pool or even an ice bucket. Great for showers, camping and kids who are rough on gadgets.

Big sound for its size

What Hi-Fi praises its punchy, cohesive sound and controlled bass, and Tom's Guide found it stayed clean even at higher volume. TechRadar says half volume was enough for a kitchen. An app that lets you shape the sound

The free JBL Portable app adds a five-band custom EQ, presets, firmware updates and a battery readout. SoundGuys, TechRadar and What Hi-Fi all call out the EQ as a real upgrade over the older Go 3, and it's the quickest way to dial in more warmth or tame the midrange to suit your music.

Easy to expand with more speakers

Thanks to Auracast, you can pair two Go 4s for stereo or link the speaker with other Auracast-ready JBL models for wider sound at a party. Tom's Guide found the app made linking simple, and SoundGuys notes the Go 4 can also stay connected to two phones or tablets at once, so switching between your music and a friend's playlist is painless.

How to get the most out of it

  • Lay it on its back for groups. TechRadar found it sounded best lying flat, firing upward, and JBL gave it textured feet so it stays steady that way.
  • Tune the EQ once. Spend two minutes in the JBL Portable app. Several reviewers found the default tuning a little flat or mid-heavy, and a small bass lift makes a big difference.
  • Save Playtime Boost for emergencies. It adds up to two hours, but What Hi-Fi and SoundGuys both note it thins the sound and switches off your custom EQ.
  • Add a carabiner. The built-in loop is small and fixed, so TechRadar and Tom's Guide suggest clipping it to a bag with a carabiner or strap.

FAQ

Is the JBL Go 4 waterproof?

Yes. It carries an IP67 rating, so it's dustproof and can handle being dropped in shallow water, making it a good fit for the pool, beach or shower.

How long does the battery last?

JBL rates it for up to 7 hours, with Playtime Boost adding up to 2 more. Actual time depends on volume and EQ settings, and a full charge over USB-C takes about 3 hours.

Can I pair two JBL Go 4 speakers?

Yes. Two Go 4s can pair for stereo, and Auracast lets it link with other compatible JBL speakers. The JBL Portable app makes setup easier.

Can I take calls on the JBL Go 4?

No. The Go 4 has no built-in microphone, so it's designed for music, podcasts and videos rather than speakerphone calls.
