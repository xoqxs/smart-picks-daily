How to get the most out of it

  • Pack it with ice. Fill it to the top with ice before adding water; the more ice you start with, the longer your drink stays cold.
  • Rotate to full cover on the move. Twist the lid to the closed position before walking around or driving to keep splashes inside.
  • Give the lid extra attention. The cup is dishwasher safe, but reviewers recommend hand-scrubbing the lid's moving parts and straw now and then so nothing lingers in the crevices.
  • Keep it upright in bags. Stand it in a side pocket or tote rather than laying it flat.
  • Use it as a daily water tracker. Two to three refills of 40 oz covers a big chunk of the day, which makes goals easy to count.
