Why people love it

Strong breeze from a tiny footprint

The Nomad One stands about three feet tall but takes up less floor space than a laundry basket, so it slips between a nightstand and the wall or beside a desk. BestReviews credits its full-length impeller with strong airflow for its size, noting its roughly 100-square-foot test room felt noticeably fresher within five minutes on the top setting. Top Ten Reviews likewise calls out its powerful output in a compact frame.

Quiet enough for bedrooms and calls

Noise is where this fan shines. In BestReviews' testing, its loudest setting peaked at 50 dB, the lowest maximum of the six towers compared, and the lowest speed was barely above the room's background level. BGR and Apartment Therapy both describe it as remarkably quiet, so it can run through a video meeting or a late-night show without stealing the spotlight.

A sleep mode that winds down with you

Hit Sleep and the display goes dark, the beeps go silent and the fan gradually eases its speed down. BestReviews said the feature "worked like a charm," and Apartment Therapy points to sleep mode as a favorite among owners. Add the 8-hour timer and the fan shuts itself off after you've drifted away.

Cleaning that doesn't require a screwdriver

Tower fans are notorious dust collectors, but the Nomad One's rear grille and impeller pop off so you can reach the inside. Dreo, BGR and Top Ten Reviews all spotlight this design, and BestReviews confirms the parts detach for a thorough dusting. A quick wipe every few weeks keeps it looking and running like new.

Set-and-forget controls

Auto mode adjusts the fan's speed to the room's temperature, a feature BestReviews and BGR both describe. The touch panel is bright and responsive, and the remote stores neatly in the rear handle, so it doesn't vanish into the couch cushions. BGR also notes the panel turns its display off after a minute, which keeps the glow out of your eyes.

How to get the most out of it

  • Give it breathing room. Air enters from the sides and back, so BestReviews recommends leaving space around the fan instead of pushing it flush against a wall or curtain.
  • Aim it with oscillation. Top Ten Reviews suggests pointing it at a seated person for a direct breeze or tucking it in a corner and letting the 90° sweep move air across the room.
  • Try Natural mode on lazy afternoons. It varies the speed to mimic a gusty breeze. BestReviews notes not everyone likes it, but it's worth a test for porch-like airflow indoors.
  • Dust it on a schedule. Pull the grille and impeller every few weeks and wipe them down, and use a soft cloth on the touch panel as BestReviews advises.
  • Carry it by the handle with care. ShopSavvy's roundup notes the remote can slide out of its slot when you move the fan, so pocket the remote first.
