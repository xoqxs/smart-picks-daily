How to get the most out of it

  • Use the right port. Plug into a USB 3.2 Gen 2 (10Gbps) or faster USB-C port for full speed; older USB ports will work but more slowly.
  • Use the included cables. Reviewers praise them; a random charging cable may limit transfer speeds.
  • Turn on password protection if the drive will carry work or personal documents.
  • Make it your backup drive. Point Time Machine or Windows backup at it so your files are protected automatically.
  • Free up your phone. On compatible phones, connect over USB-C and move videos straight off to reclaim space.

FAQ

Does the Samsung T7 work with iPhone?

Samsung lists compatibility with iPhone 15 and newer, which use USB-C. Connect it with the included USB-C to C cable.

How fast is the Samsung T7?

Samsung rates it at up to 1,050 MB/s read and 1,000 MB/s write over USB 3.2 Gen 2. Real speeds depend on your device's port.

What cables come in the box?

Two: USB-C to USB-C and USB-C to USB-A, so it works with both new and older computers.

What's the difference between the T7 and T7 Shield?

The T7 Shield adds a rubber exterior and an IP65 dust and water resistance rating for tougher conditions. The standard T7 is slimmer and lighter with an all-aluminum body.
