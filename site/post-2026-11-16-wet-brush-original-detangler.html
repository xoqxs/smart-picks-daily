How to get the most out of it

  • Brush with conditioner in. Work it through your hair in the shower while conditioner is still in for the smoothest glide.
  • Start at the ends. Detangle the bottom few inches first, then work your way up toward the roots to avoid pushing knots together.
  • Go section by section. On thick or curly hair, clip hair into sections and detangle one at a time.
  • Use it dry for quick touch-ups. It's great for smoothing flyaways before styling or putting hair up.
  • Keep it clean. Pull out trapped hair regularly and rinse the brush so the bristles stay springy.
