How to get the most out of it

  • Start small. Warm one or two drops between your palms; add more only if your ends still look dry.
  • Mid-lengths to ends, not roots. Keeping it off your scalp area keeps hair feeling light and bouncy.
  • Apply to towel-dried hair, then comb through. Moroccanoil recommends using it before you blow-dry or let hair air-dry.
  • Finish with a touch. On dry hair, a tiny amount smooths flyaways and adds a final gloss.
  • Boost your mask. The brand suggests mixing a few drops into your hair mask for extra softness.

FAQ

Is Moroccanoil Treatment pure argan oil?

No. It's an argan oil-infused formula. Its base includes silicones (cyclomethicone and dimethicone), which is what gives that smooth, glossy slip.

Should I use it on wet or dry hair?

Both work. Use it on towel-dried hair before styling, or a small amount on dry hair to smooth flyaways and finish your look.

Which Moroccanoil Treatment is best for fine hair?

Moroccanoil recommends Treatment Light for fine or light-colored hair, and Treatment Original for medium to thick hair.

How much should I use?

Start with one or two drops (about one pump) for shoulder-length hair and adjust. Longer or thicker hair may need a little more.
