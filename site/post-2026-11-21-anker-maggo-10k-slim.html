How to get the most out of it

  • Plug in when you're in a hurry. Wired charging through the USB-C port was clearly faster than wireless in Digital Camera World's testing, so use a cable when you need a big boost quickly and save the magnetic pad for top-ups on the move.
  • Refill it with a 30W charger. Both TechRadar and Digital Camera World note the bank recharges in about two to two and a half hours with a 30W-capable adapter and cable. Plugging it in overnight works just as well.
