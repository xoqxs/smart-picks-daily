How to get the most out of it

  • Warm up the colors. Reviewers found the default image can lean slightly cool. Open Logitech's free software (Logi Tune or G HUB), switch off auto white balance and nudge it warmer until skin tones look natural.
  • Face the light. Put a window or lamp in front of you, not behind you. RightLight helps, but a soft front light makes the biggest difference.
  • Set it at eye level. Clip it to the top center of your monitor, or use the tripod thread with a small stand, so you're looking at people instead of down at them.
  • Pair it with a better mic for streaming. The dual mics are handy for calls, but a headset or USB mic will make your voice sound fuller on recordings.
  • Choose 1080p in your app. Some apps default to lower resolutions; check the video settings in Zoom, Teams or OBS so you get the full HD picture.
