How to get the most out of it

  • Give it some distance. Holding the bottle a little away from your face spreads the mist more evenly instead of leaving big droplets. Close your eyes while you spray; the brand advises rinsing with water if any gets in your eyes.
  • Follow with moisturizer. Mist onto clean skin, then apply your moisturizer while your face is still slightly damp. Reviewers agree the spray works best as a partner to your moisturizer, not a replacement.
