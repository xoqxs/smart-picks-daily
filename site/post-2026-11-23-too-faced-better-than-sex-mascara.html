How to get the most out of it

  • Wiggle at the root. Too Faced recommends starting at the base of the lashes and wiggling the wand upward, which deposits more formula where it creates the most volume.
  • Try "bottoms first." The brand suggests coating lower lashes before upper ones so wet mascara doesn't transfer onto your lid when you look up.
  • Use the tip for corners. Woman & Home suggests holding the wand vertically and using its narrow tip to fan out outer lashes for a wide-eyed look.
  • Start with one coat, then decide. Who What Wear and Woman & Home both suggest a single coat for daytime and a second only when you want more drama, which keeps the look tailored to the occasion.
  • Curl first. A quick pass with a lash curler before the first coat helps the formula's curl-holding polymers keep that lift in place longer.

Can I wear Better Than Sex with contact lenses?

Too Faced states that the mascara is ophthalmologist tested and suitable for contact lens wearers.

How many coats should I apply?

One coat gives defined, natural-looking lashes, and a second coat adds more length and volume. Reviewers say the formula layers smoothly without clumping.

Does it work on short lashes?

Yes, but the full-size brush is fairly large. Reviewers suggest the travel-size version, which has a smaller wand, for short lashes or more precise application.
