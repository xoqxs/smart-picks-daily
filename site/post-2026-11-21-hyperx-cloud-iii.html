Why people love it

Comfort that disappears on your head

This is the headline feature. Thick memory foam in the cups and headband, a gentle clamp and a light frame add up to a headset that Tom's Hardware says is easy to forget you're wearing. PC Gamer and Tom's Hardware both found it comfortable over glasses, and Best Buy reviewers often describe it as comfortable for long sessions.

Sound tuned for the game

The angled 53 mm drivers deliver strong, punchy bass and crisp highs. TechRadar says the imaging is good enough to pinpoint distant enemies, and GameSpot found positional cues sharp enough for competitive shooters like Valorant. Footsteps and directional audio come up again and again in user reviews, too.

A mic your squad will thank you for

Headset mics are often an afterthought, but this one gets real praise. TechRadar says it sounds clear and does a good job of rejecting keyboard clatter, and GameSpot compared its recordings to a standalone USB mic. The built-in mesh filter helps tame harsh sounds, and the LED tells you at a glance when you're muted. When you're done chatting, the boom pops off for music or movies.

Sturdy build with a premium feel

A metal headband and aluminum forks give the Cloud III a sturdy, premium feel. GameSpot found the frame flexes and takes rough handling without complaint, and Tom's Hardware describes the build as upscale for the price. Best Buy shoppers repeatedly call it rugged, which matters if it's headed to a teenager.

How to get the most out of it

  • Raise the mic gain. Tom's Hardware and PC Gamer both found the mic records on the quiet side. Turn up the input level in Windows, Discord or NGENUITY, and position the boom close to the corner of your mouth.
  • Use USB on PC. HyperX says the USB connection on a supported Windows PC unlocks NGENUITY features such as the 10-band EQ. Treat spatial audio as a bonus: TechRadar, PC Gamer and GameSpot all describe the effect as subtle.
  • Use the ear-cup wheel. GameSpot points out that the volume wheel works independently of your system volume, so you can fine-tune loudness without leaving the game.
  • Mute between rounds, not mid-sentence. Tom's Hardware notes the mute button clicks audibly, so tap it during quiet moments rather than while teammates are talking.
  • Dial in the fit. The cups slide and extend, though Tom's Hardware and GameSpot note they don't swivel. Take a minute to adjust the height so the foam seals evenly around your ears.

FAQ

Does the HyperX Cloud III work with PS5 and Xbox?

Yes. It connects to PS5 by USB or 3.5 mm, and to Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One through the 3.5 mm jack on the controller.

Is the microphone removable?

Yes. The boom mic detaches, so you can use the Cloud III as regular headphones for music or movies.

Do I need software to use it?

No. It works as soon as you plug it in. The optional NGENUITY app on Windows adds EQ, mic and spatial audio controls when you connect by USB.

Is it comfortable with glasses?

Reviewers at Tom's Hardware and PC Gamer both found the soft memory foam pads comfortable over glasses.
