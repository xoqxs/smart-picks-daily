How to get the most out of it

  • Start with less than you think. Stylist and Brit + Co both say a little goes a long way, and the doe-foot applicator picks up plenty. Dot a few spots on your face and blend them out before you add more.
  • Blend with a sponge or brush. Stylist used a beauty sponge and Bustle used a dense brush. e.l.f.'s own guide suggests a sponge for a skin-tint look and a buffing brush when you wear it under foundation.
  • Powder your T-zone if you're oily. Marie Claire and Bustle both found oily areas looked shiny after a few hours, so keep the glow on your cheekbones and set your forehead, nose and chin with a little powder.
  • Start with a grippy primer. e.l.f. pairs Halo Glow with its Power Grip Primer for a lit-from-within look, and Brit + Co also suggests a primer underneath. A base layer gives the glow something smooth to sit on.
  • Judge the shade on your skin, not in the bottle. Stylist and Brit + Co both say the liquid can look different in the bottle than it does on skin, so swatch it on your jawline before you decide.
