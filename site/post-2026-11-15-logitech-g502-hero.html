Who it's for

PC gamers who play MMOs, shooters or strategy games and want extra thumb buttons, and anyone who uses a mouse for editing or spreadsheets and likes binding shortcuts to buttons. If you want the lightest mouse possible for fast flick shots, this isn't it: the G502 is built around adjustability, not minimal weight.

Three features that matter day to day

Tunable weight. Five 3.6 g weights fit into the mouse in different front, back, left, right and center positions, so you can shift the balance instead of just adding mass.

Onboard memory. Set your DPI and button mapping once in Logitech G HUB, save it to the mouse, and it works on another PC without installing software.

A wide DPI range. Logitech rates the HERO 25K sensor from 100 to 25,600 DPI with no smoothing, filtering or acceleration. Most people end up between 800 and 3,200, but the range gives you room to experiment.

One tip

Start with fewer weights than you think. Install G HUB, play for a day with just one or two weights in the center, then add more only if the mouse feels too floaty. It's easier to notice the difference when you change one thing at a time.
