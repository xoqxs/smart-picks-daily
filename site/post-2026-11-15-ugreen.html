Mistake 1: Ignoring the wattage

Old 5W cube chargers are still everywhere. A modern phone can draw far more than that, so an old brick quietly caps your charging speed. Look at the number printed on the charger: for a phone, aim for 20W or more with USB-C Power Delivery (PD). For a laptop, you'll want around 60–65W.

Mistake 2: Forgetting the cable

Your charging chain is only as fast as its weakest link. A 100W charger plugged into a cable rated for 60W will only ever deliver 60W. If you charge a laptop, use a USB-C cable rated 100W or 240W.

Mistake 3: Buying a bulky, old-style charger

Newer chargers use GaN (gallium nitride) parts instead of traditional silicon. In practice that means the same power in a much smaller body, which matters if the charger lives in your bag.

Quick checklist

  • Phone: 20W+ USB-C PD charger
  • Laptop: 60–65W+ charger and a 100W-rated cable
  • Carry it around? Pick GaN
