How to get the most out of it

  • Turn on your 2.4GHz network first. Govee notes the bulbs don't support 5GHz Wi-Fi, so make sure your router's 2.4GHz band is on (and named clearly) before setup.
  • Use white for tasks, color for mood. TechRadar observed that saturated colors look dimmer than white, and TODAY's reviewer said it's not the brightest bulb in its test. Lean on tunable white for reading or cooking and save the rainbow for ambiance.
  • Group bulbs by room. Both Govee and TechRadar point to room grouping, so one tap or one voice command can change every lamp in the living room at once.
  • Keep the wall switch on. Govee says these bulbs aren't compatible with smart switches, so leave the regular switch powered and control the light from the app or your voice assistant.
  • Build a cozy routine. Set a warm, dimmed scene for the last hour before bed, then add a sunrise-style schedule for mornings. Since the bulbs follow the schedule automatically, the room is ready before you are.

FAQ

Do Govee smart light bulbs need a hub?

No. The H6008 bulbs connect directly to your home Wi-Fi and pair with your phone over Bluetooth, so all you need is the free Govee Home app.

Do Govee bulbs work with Alexa and Google Home?

Yes. Govee lists support for Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant, so you can turn the bulbs on and off and change color or brightness by voice.

Do Govee bulbs work with 5GHz Wi-Fi?

No. Govee states the bulbs use 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, so keep your router's 2.4GHz band turned on during setup.

Can I use Govee bulbs with a dimmer or smart switch?

Govee says these bulbs are not compatible with smart switches. Leave the wall switch on and dim them from the app or with your voice assistant instead.
