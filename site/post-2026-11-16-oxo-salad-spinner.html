How to get the most out of it

  • Wash by soaking. Set the basket in the bowl, fill with water to cover the greens, swish, then lift the basket and pour out the gritty water. InsideHook calls this the step most people skip.
  • Don't overfill. Spin in two smaller batches instead of one packed basket for the driest leaves.
  • Pour off water between spins. Empty the bowl after the first round and give it a few more pumps.
  • Try it on berries and herbs. A gentle spin dries delicate fruit and herbs without bruising them by hand.
  • Brake before you lift. Tap the brake button and wait a beat so the basket is fully stopped before you open the lid.
