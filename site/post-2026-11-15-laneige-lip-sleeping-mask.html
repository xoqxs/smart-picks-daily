Who it's for

This is for people whose lips look fine at lunch but feel tight and flaky when they wake up. It also suits anyone who reapplies balm all day and wants one heavier layer that stays put while they sleep. If you only want a quick swipe of gloss on the go, a tube balm is a better fit (we cover one of those in Pick #5).

Three things worth knowing

It's a mask, not a gloss. The texture is thicker than a normal balm because it's meant to sit on your lips for hours. A thin, even layer is enough.

The formula leans on butters. LANEIGE lists shea butter and murumuru seed butter along with its Berry Fruit Complex, a combination aimed at moisture and softness rather than color.

It's a long-running bestseller. LANEIGE says it has been the #1 lip treatment brand in the US for four years in a row (based on Circana prestige beauty data), and the Amazon listing has tens of thousands of ratings.
