How to get the most out of it

  • Vacuum first. Run a regular vacuum over the area to lift loose dirt and hair before you go wet. Reviewers and a professional upholstery cleaner on Reddit both point out this keeps the tool from clogging and gives better results.
  • Give the formula a few minutes. Spray, scrub lightly, then let it sit for roughly 5 to 10 minutes before suctioning. Tough or set-in stains respond much better than with a quick spray-and-suck.
  • Use warm or hot tap water, never boiling. Fill to the formula line, then top off with hot tap water. Boiling water can damage the plastic parts.
  • Check the fabric first. Look for the cleaning code on furniture tags and test a hidden spot, especially on delicate or natural fabrics, before tackling a visible cushion.
  • Rinse and dry. Run the HydroRinse tool for about 30 seconds after every session to flush the hose, empty the dirty tank, and point a fan at the cleaned area so it dries faster.

FAQ

Can the Little Green clean car seats?

Yes. BISSELL lists auto interiors as a supported surface, and the crevice tool helps with tight spots around seats and floor mats.

Does the Little Green heat the water?

No, the 1400B doesn't have a heater. Fill it with hot tap water (not boiling). The ProHeat version helps keep hot water warm, and the HydroSteam has a built-in heater.

How long does fabric take to dry afterward?

Light spot cleaning can dry in an hour or two, while heavier jobs can take several hours. A fan or open window speeds things up.

Can I clean a whole room of carpet with it?

It's designed for spots, stairs, upholstery and small rugs. For whole rooms, a full-size upright carpet cleaner will save you a lot of time and bending.
