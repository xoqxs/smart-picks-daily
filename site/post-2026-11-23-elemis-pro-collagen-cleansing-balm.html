How to get the most out of it

  • Start on dry skin. Take a small amount with clean, dry fingers and massage it over dry skin and makeup for about a minute.
  • Add water to emulsify. Wet your fingertips and keep massaging until the balm turns milky. This is what lifts everything away.
  • Remove with a warm cloth. ELEMIS and reviewers alike recommend a warm, damp washcloth for a clean, polished finish.
  • Make it step one of a double cleanse. Marie Claire and HuffPost suggest following with a water-based cleanser if you have oily skin or wear heavy sunscreen. Our pick, the CeraVe Hydrating Facial Cleanser, is a gentle second step.
