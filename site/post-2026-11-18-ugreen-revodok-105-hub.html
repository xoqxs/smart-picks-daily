How to get the most out of it

  • Check your laptop's USB-C port first. HDMI only works if your laptop's USB-C port supports video output (DisplayPort Alt Mode or Thunderbolt). Most MacBooks and many recent Windows laptops do; your spec sheet will say.
  • Use a strong enough charger. The hub uses a little power for itself, so plug in a USB-C charger rated a bit above what your laptop normally needs to avoid "slow charger" warnings.
  • Put drives on the blue port. SSDs and flash drives belong on the USB 3.0 port; keyboards, mice and receivers are happy on the USB 2.0 ports.
  • Keep power steady during big jobs. UGREEN advises against plugging or unplugging the charger mid-transfer or while screen sharing, so connect power first.
  • Set your display to 1080p if 4K feels sluggish. At 1080p the picture stays smooth and sharp for everyday work.

Does the UGREEN Revodok 105 work with a MacBook?

Can it charge my laptop?

Yes. Connect your USB-C charger to the hub's PD port and it passes up to 100W through to your laptop. A charger isn't included.

Does the HDMI port support 4K?

It outputs up to 4K at 30Hz. That's great for documents and video; gamers and fast-scrolling users may prefer a 4K 60Hz hub.

Can I use the USB-C port on the hub for data?

No. That USB-C port is for charging input only; use the USB-A ports for drives and accessories.
