How to get the most out of it

  • Patch test first. Shop TODAY and ShopSavvy both recommend trying it on a small area and waiting a day before using it all over, which is smart with any new active.
  • Use 3 to 5 drops. That's the brand's suggested amount, and reviewers agree more isn't better. Press it into clean skin, including the neck if you like.
  • Lock it in with moisturizer. If your skin runs dry, follow with a moisturizer or mix a couple of drops into your cream. PureWow found that combination left skin smoother and more hydrated.
  • Make it a morning habit, then add sunscreen. Reviewers at Shop TODAY and CNN Underscored apply it in the morning and finish with SPF before makeup, so it fits neatly into a daytime routine.
  • Be consistent. The brighter look builds over weeks of daily use, so give it time before judging.
