How to get the most out of it

  • Hand wash when you can. Victorinox lists the knife as dishwasher safe, but Food Network recommends hand washing to protect the edge and keep it sharp longer. A quick rinse, soap and towel dry takes seconds.
  • Hone often, sharpen occasionally. A few passes on a honing steel before cooking keeps the edge aligned, and an occasional sharpening restores it, as both Victorinox and long-time owners suggest.
  • Pair it with a good board. A wood or plastic cutting board is kinder to the edge than glass, stone or ceramic surfaces.
  • Let the blade do the work on dense foods. For sweet potatoes or winter squash, Food Network found a bit of downward pressure helps, so cut them into flat-sided pieces first for steadier, safer slices.
  • Store it safely. A block, magnetic strip or blade guard keeps the edge from banging around in a drawer.
