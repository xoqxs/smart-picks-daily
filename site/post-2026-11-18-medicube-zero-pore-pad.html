How to get the most out of it

  • Cleanse first, then swipe in order. Start with the embossed side, sweeping from the center of the face outward, then follow with the silky side and pat in what's left. Medicube, The Quality Edit and AOL all recommend this sequence.
  • Ease in. Begin with a few nights a week and build up as your skin gets used to the acids. If your skin is oily, you may enjoy more frequent use; if it's sensitive, keep it occasional.
  • Keep other exfoliants on separate nights. The Quality Edit and AOL both advise against using the pads at the same time as retinol or another acid product. Many people use the pads in the morning and save retinoids for the evening.
  • Seal it in. Medicube suggests following with a serum or moisturizer if your skin feels dry, and a cream also takes care of the slightly tacky finish the silky side can leave.
  • Focus on the oily zones. If your cheeks are drier, concentrate the embossed side on your forehead, nose and chin, and use the silky side everywhere else.
