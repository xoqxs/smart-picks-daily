How to get the most out of it

  • Press, don't sweep. Laura Mercier recommends loading a velour puff, tapping off the excess and pressing it onto skin with a rolling motion. Better Homes & Gardens suggests the same pressing idea with a damp makeup sponge. Pressing sets makeup without moving it around.
  • Focus on where you get shiny. Concentrate on the T-zone and around the nose, and go lighter on the outer cheeks. The brand suggests a second, firmer layer on shine-prone spots.
  • Use a small brush for tricky corners. For the sides of the nose and under the lower lash line, a small tapered brush gives more control than a big puff.
  • Try the lipstick trick. Laura Mercier suggests placing a tissue over your lips and pressing a little powder through it to help lipstick last longer and transfer less.
