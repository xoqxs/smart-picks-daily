Who it's for

Pick the Clip 5 if you want music for hikes, bike rides, beach days, the shower or the garage, and you'd rather not carry anything in your hand. If you mostly listen at home and want room-filling sound, a larger speaker such as JBL's Flip or Charge models is the better fit. The Clip trades size for go-anywhere convenience.

What changed in the fifth generation

Easier carabiner. JBL redesigned the clip with a bigger, more accessible opening, so it goes over thick straps and bars more easily.

More driver power. JBL says the Clip 5 gets an increase in driver power for more consistent bass across volume levels.

Auracast. Instead of older proprietary pairing, the Clip 5 supports Auracast, which lets you link it with other Auracast-enabled JBL portables for stereo or multi-speaker sound.

The detail to check before you buy

If you already own a JBL speaker and plan to pair the two, check whether your current model supports Auracast. JBL introduced it to its portables with the Clip 5, Go 4 and Xtreme 4 generation, so speakers from earlier lineups may not link with it. As a single speaker, it simply connects to your phone over Bluetooth like any other.

Quick checklist

  • Music outdoors or in the shower? Clip 5 fits.
  • Want big living-room sound? Size up instead.
  • Pairing with another JBL? Confirm it supports Auracast.
