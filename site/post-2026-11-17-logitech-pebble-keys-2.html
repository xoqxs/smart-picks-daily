FAQ

Does the K380s work with iPad?

Yes. It connects over Bluetooth to iPadOS and iOS, as well as Windows, macOS, Android and ChromeOS.

Is the K380s rechargeable?

No. It runs on two AAA batteries, which are included, and Logitech rates battery life at up to 36 months.

Does it come with a USB receiver?

No. It connects by Bluetooth out of the box. It's compatible with Logitech's Logi Bolt receiver, which is sold separately.

What's the difference between the K380 and K380s?

The K380s adds longer battery life (up to 36 months versus 24), customizable shortcut keys through Logi Options+ and a refreshed design with recycled plastic.
