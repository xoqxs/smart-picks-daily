FAQ

Is Neutrogena Hydro Boost Water Gel good for oily skin?

Yes. It's formulated for normal, oily and combination skin, absorbs quickly and is labeled non-comedogenic, so it hydrates without a greasy feel.

Can I wear it under makeup?

Yes. It sinks in fast, so it works well as a base under sunscreen and foundation. Give it about a minute to settle first.

Does it contain fragrance?

The classic Water Gel jar contains fragrance. If you prefer fragrance-free, choose the Hydro Boost Gel-Cream Fragrance Free.

What's the difference between the Water Gel and the Gel-Cream?

The Water Gel is lighter and aimed at normal-to-oily skin; the Gel-Cream is fragrance-free, slightly richer and aimed at normal-to-dry skin.
