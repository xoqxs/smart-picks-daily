How to get the most out of it

  • Pick the right ANC mode. Use Transport on planes and trains, Outdoor on windy walks and Indoor at your desk; you switch modes in the soundcore app.
  • Tune the EQ once. Spend two minutes in the app to tame the bass or try a preset; you'll hear a big difference.
  • Use transparency for quick chats. A press of the NC button lets outside sound in, handy for airport announcements or coffee orders.
  • Pair two devices. Connect your laptop and phone together with multipoint so calls and music follow you.
  • Keep the cable in your bag. The 3.5mm aux option is perfect for in-flight entertainment screens.
