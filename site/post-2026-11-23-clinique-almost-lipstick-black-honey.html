Does Clinique Black Honey look good on everyone?

It's designed to. The sheer formula blends with your natural lip color, so the result looks a little different on each person. Clinique, Who What Wear and Yahoo's shopping team all describe it as flattering across skin tones.

Is Black Honey a lipstick or a gloss?

It's a hybrid. Clinique positions Almost Lipstick between the two: it has the slim bullet of a lipstick with a sheer, soft-shine finish and a lightweight, balm-like feel.

Is Clinique Almost Lipstick fragrance free?

Yes. Clinique lists it as allergy tested and fragrance free, and also free of parabens and phthalates.

How do I make Black Honey look darker?
