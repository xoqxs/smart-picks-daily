How to get the most out of it

  • Put big adapters on the outside. A Woot community owner noted that large bricks fit well on the outer outlets but can crowd the inner ones, so save the middle column for slim plugs and cords.
  • Use USB-C alone for the fastest phone top-up. Anker says the full 20W is available only when the USB-A ports aren't in use; with everything plugged in, the USB ports share 15W.
  • Plug it straight into the wall. Anker's buying guide advises against daisy-chaining strips and recommends keeping high-draw appliances like space heaters and hair dryers on their own wall outlet.
  • Mount it before you cable-manage. Fix the strip under the desk first, then route cords to it, and the setup stays tidy when you move things around.
