How to get the most out of it

  • Find the right ear tips first. The medium tips come pre-installed, but a good seal makes a big difference for both bass and noise cancelling. HiFi Report and MyChooz both stress fit, so try the small and large tips too.
  • Tweak the EQ in the soundcore app. If vocals or podcasts sound a little muddy, pick a preset or build a custom EQ to tame the bass.
  • Customize or simplify the touch controls. HiFi Report and Robb Sutton both found the touch controls easy to trigger by accident. Remap them in the app so a stray tap while adjusting the buds doesn't pause your music.
  • Use multipoint to pair two devices. Connect your phone and laptop at the same time so you can hop from a playlist to a video call without re-pairing.
  • Keep a USB-C charger handy. The case charges over USB-C and doesn't support wireless charging pads, a point Robb Sutton and Best Buy shoppers both mention, so plug it in alongside your phone at night.
