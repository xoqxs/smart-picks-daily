How to get the most out of it

  • Warm the porcelain first. Eight Ounce Coffee and pour-over fans on Reddit both suggest preheating a ceramic dripper with hot water, which also rinses the paper taste out of the filter.
  • Pour with a gooseneck kettle. Tom's Guide and NBC Select's experts both recommend a gooseneck spout for slow, steady circles. Our COSORI Gooseneck Kettle pick is a great partner here.
  • Weigh your coffee. A kitchen scale makes your favorite recipe repeatable. Tom's Guide suggests starting near a 1:16 coffee-to-water ratio, and the Etekcity EK6015 scale handles the job neatly.
  • Start with a bloom. Wet the grounds with a small splash of water, wait briefly while they puff up, then continue in stages, aiming between the filter wall and the coffee bed.
  • Stock the right filters. Use V60 size 02 paper filters. NBC Select notes they can be hard to find in stores, so order a pack alongside the dripper.

Hario rates the size 02 dripper for one to four cups. In practice it comfortably fills one or two large mugs or a small carafe.

Is the ceramic V60 dishwasher safe?
