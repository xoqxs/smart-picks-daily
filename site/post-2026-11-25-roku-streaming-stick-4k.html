FAQ

Is the Roku Streaming Stick 4K worth it?

For most people with a 4K TV, yes. It adds Dolby Vision, HDR10+, a long-range Wi-Fi receiver and Roku's easy interface in a stick that hides behind the screen, which is why reviewers like Tom's Guide and Reviewed rate it so highly.

What is the difference between the Roku Streaming Stick 4K and the Streaming Stick Plus?

Both stream in 4K with HDR10+ and HLG. The Streaming Stick 4K adds Dolby Vision and a long-range Wi-Fi receiver, while the newer Streaming Stick Plus is smaller and simpler, without Dolby Vision.

Can the Roku Streaming Stick 4K be powered by the TV's USB port?

Often, yes, but not every TV supplies enough power. If the stick won't stay on, use the power adapter that comes in the box.

Does the Roku Streaming Stick 4K work with Alexa and Apple devices?

Yes. Roku lists support for Apple AirPlay and HomeKit, Amazon Alexa and Google Home, so you can cast from an iPhone or control playback with a compatible smart speaker.
