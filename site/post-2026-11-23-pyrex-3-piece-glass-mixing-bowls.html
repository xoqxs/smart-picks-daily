How to get the most out of it

  • Match the bowl to the job. Use the 1-quart for eggs, melted butter and small sauces, the 1.5-quart for dressings and dips, and the 2.5-quart for batters, salads and doughs.
  • Microwave with care. Pyrex advises against heating an empty or nearly empty bowl and recommends the minimum cook time when melting butter or warming oil.
  • Let hot glass cool down first. Pyrex says to let warm glassware cool before washing, chilling or freezing, and not to set a hot bowl directly on a countertop, metal surface or in the sink.
  • Keep it off direct heat. Pyrex's glassware instructions rule out stovetops, broilers and open flames. For a double boiler, a metal bowl is the better tool.
  • Load the dishwasher thoughtfully. Pyrex recommends placing glass so it won't bump other items during the cycle, and using non-abrasive cleaners for hand washing.
