How to get the most out of it

  • Preheat until the light goes out. Presto's manual says the indicator light switches off when the griddle reaches the set temperature, usually after about 5 to 10 minutes. Starting batter before then means pale, uneven pancakes.
  • Use the right heat for each food. Presto's cooking chart suggests roughly 350–400°F for pancakes, 325–350°F for bacon and 275–300°F for fried eggs. Start bacon first, then turn the dial up for pancakes.
  • Choose soft utensils. Presto recommends nylon, plastic, wooden or rubber tools and warns against metal utensils or cutting on the surface, which protects the ceramic nonstick coating.
  • Skip the dishwasher and the spray. The manual says to hand wash it and skip cooking sprays, which can leave the surface tacky. Both TechGearLab and Feelgoodfoodie found the textured top takes a little more wiping than a smooth one, so a soft sponge and warm soapy water work best.
  • Let it cool before washing. Feelgoodfoodie and TechGearLab both suggest waiting until the griddle and tray are cool before rinsing them.

FAQ

How big is the cooking surface on the Presto 22-inch electric griddle?

Presto's spec sheet lists a cooking area of about 22 by 11.5 inches, which is enough room for pancakes, bacon and eggs at the same time.

What temperature should an electric griddle be for pancakes?

Presto's cooking chart for this griddle suggests 350–400°F for pancakes, cooked for about 2 to 3 minutes. Wait until the indicator light goes out before pouring the batter.

Can you put the Presto griddle in water?

Yes. Presto says the griddle is fully immersible once the heat control is detached. Never put the heat control itself in water.

Is the Presto 22-inch griddle dishwasher safe?

Presto's instructions say to hand wash the griddle in warm, soapy water instead of using a dishwasher, because repeated dishwasher cycles can reduce nonstick performance.
