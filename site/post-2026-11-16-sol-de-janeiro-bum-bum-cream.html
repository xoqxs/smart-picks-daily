FAQ

What does Brazilian Bum Bum Cream smell like?

Sol de Janeiro describes the Cheirosa 62 scent as pistachio and almond up top, jasmine and heliotrope in the middle, and vanilla, salted caramel and sandalwood in the dry-down. Most people describe it as sweet, warm and beachy.

Can I use it on my face?

Sol de Janeiro says the cream hasn't been tested for use on the face, so keep it for the body and use a dedicated facial moisturizer above the neck.

Does it contain nuts?

Yes. The brand lists Brazil nut among its ingredients, so anyone with a nut allergy should check the full ingredient list before buying.

Which size should I start with?

If you already know you love the scent, go straight for the 240 mL jar. If you're curious, the 75 mL size is a lower-commitment way to try it and doubles as a travel size.
