How to get the most out of it

  • Boil plain water first. Chefman recommends running one full kettle of water and discarding it before first use.
  • Add the infuser after heating. Wait until the kettle reaches your temperature, then add the leaves and steep for about 3 to 7 minutes, depending on the tea. Lift it out carefully; it's hot.
  • Stay between the MIN and MAX lines. Filling above 1.8 liters can make it spit while boiling.
  • Descale with white vinegar. When you see mineral spots, boil a small amount of vinegar, repeat if needed, then rinse well. Regular descaling keeps the glass looking clear.
  • Keep the base dry. Never submerge the kettle or put it in the dishwasher; wipe the outside and base with a damp cloth and dry it.
  • Use a long brush for the inside. The opening is narrow, so a bottle brush makes cleaning easier.

FAQ

What temperatures does the Chefman kettle have?

Five presets: 175°F (green tea), 185°F (white tea), 195°F (oolong), 200°F (coffee) and 212°F (black and herbal tea). There's no custom temperature setting between them.

Does it have a keep-warm function?

Yes. On the 212°F setting, hold the power button for two seconds to keep the water warm after it boils.

Is any part dishwasher safe?

No. Chefman says to hand-wash the infuser and lid and to wipe the kettle and base with a damp cloth. Don't submerge the kettle.

Will it turn on if it's empty?

It has boil-dry protection, so it shuts off if it runs dry and resets once it cools.
