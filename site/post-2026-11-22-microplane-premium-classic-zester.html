How to get the most out of it

  • Use a light touch. Reviewed notes it takes very little pressure, and BestReviews suggests gentle strokes in one direction instead of sawing back and forth. Let the blade do the work.
  • Rinse it right away. MetaFilter owners and TechGearLab both say cheese and citrus residue are much easier to remove before they dry. A quick rinse, or a run through the dishwasher, keeps the teeth clear.
  • Tap off what clings. Sticky foods like ginger and garlic can collect on the underside, as America's Test Kitchen observed. A firm tap against the edge of the bowl, as Reviewed suggests, releases most of it.
  • Put the cover back on. The blade is very sharp, and Reviewed and BestReviews both highlight the cover as the easy way to store it safely.

FAQ

What can you grate with a Microplane zester?

Citrus peel, hard cheeses like Parmesan, garlic, fresh ginger, whole nutmeg, cinnamon and chocolate are the most common uses. Softer cheeses and large vegetable batches are better suited to a box grater.

Is the Microplane Premium Classic dishwasher safe?

Yes. Microplane lists it as dishwasher safe. Rinsing it soon after use makes cleanup even easier, since dried cheese and citrus are harder to remove.

What is the difference between the Premium Classic and the Classic?

Both use a long stainless steel rasp blade. The Premium Classic adds a soft-touch handle, non-scratch end tabs and rubber feet, while the original Classic 40020 has a hard plastic handle.

Does it come with a cover?

Yes. A reusable protective cover is included to guard the blade in a drawer.
