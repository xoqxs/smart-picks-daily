How to get the most out of it

  • Tare between every ingredient. Add each ingredient to the same bowl and press the tare button in between. It's faster than measuring cups and cuts down on dishes.
  • Use mL mode for water and milk. It's calibrated for water-like liquids, so treat readings for oil, honey or syrup as close estimates and switch to grams if your recipe lists weights.
  • Wipe, don't rinse. Clean the top with a damp cloth and keep the scale away from running water. Food Network notes that moisture near the battery area is the one thing to avoid.
  • Keep it awake during slow prep. The display turns itself off to save battery. If you're chopping between additions, tap a button now and then.
  • Mind big platters. A wide plate can hang over the display. A bowl or a smaller board keeps the numbers visible.
