How to get the most out of it

  • Get close. FIFINE recommends keeping the mic about 3 inches from your mouth with the gain dial set between 50% and 75%. Talking into the front, not the top, gives the clearest sound.
  • Point it away from noise. It's a cardioid mic, so aim the front at you and the back toward your keyboard or PC fan. Geekman suggests a noise gate in your streaming software to cut out clicks.
  • Widen the tripod. MKAU Gaming and Explosion Network both found the light tripod can tip if it isn't set up carefully, so spread the legs wide, or move the mic to a boom arm using the included adapter.
  • Wear headphones to stop echo. FIFINE says headphones keep the mic from picking up your speakers. Our Sony WH-CH520 (a past pick) is a comfortable, lightweight match.
  • Turn off auto volume. If your level keeps jumping around, FIFINE advises disabling automatic gain in Discord or your system sound settings.
