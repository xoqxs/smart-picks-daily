Yes. It connects with a 3.5 mm jack, and Razer lists support for PC, Mac, PS5, Xbox, Nintendo Switch, Steam Deck and mobile devices. On consoles, you plug it into the controller or the device's headphone jack.

Yes, on Windows PCs. Razer's software adds virtual 7.1 surround sound. On consoles and other devices, it plays regular stereo audio.

No. The HyperClear Cardioid mic is fixed, but it sits on a bendable boom, so you can move it out of the way when you're not talking.
