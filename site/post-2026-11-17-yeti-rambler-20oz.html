How to get the most out of it

  • Keep the slider closed. Outside found heat retention roughly halved with the slider left open, so close it between sips.
  • Deep clean the lid once a week. Lift off the magnetic slider and pinch out the rubber gasket, wash, then let every piece dry before reassembling.
  • Load the dishwasher the YETI way. Lid on the top rack, gasket and slider in the utensil basket so they don't get lost.
  • Lift coffee stains easily. A YETI cleaning tablet (or a similar tumbler tablet) dissolved in warm water for a few minutes brightens the inside.
  • Keep it out of the microwave. It's stainless steel; heat your drink first, then pour.

FAQ

Is the YETI Rambler 20 oz dishwasher safe?

Yes. YETI says the tumbler and all lid parts can go in the dishwasher; put the lid on the top rack and the small parts in the utensil basket.

Is the MagSlider lid leakproof?

No. It's designed to reduce splashes while you sip, and YETI notes it won't prevent spills if the cup tips over or rides in a bag.

Can I use a straw with it?

Yes, with YETI's separately sold Straw Lid. YETI says that lid is for cold drinks only.

Does it fit in a car cupholder?

YETI says the 20 oz fits most cupholders, and most reviewers agree, though very narrow holders can be snug.
