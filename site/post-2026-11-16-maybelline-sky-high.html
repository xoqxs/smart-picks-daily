How to get the most out of it

  • Start at the roots. Press the brush into the base of your lashes, then sweep up to the tips. That's where most of the lift comes from.
  • Build in thin layers. Expect the first coat to look subtle. Add a second or third coat right after the first for more length. Reviewed found that stopping at about three coats avoids clumping.
  • Use the tapered tip for small lashes. The narrow end of the brush makes it easy to reach the short lashes at the inner corners.
  • Remove it gently. Maybelline recommends micellar water or a gentle eye makeup remover. Reviewers also had good results with a cleansing balm or oil.

FAQ

Is Maybelline Sky High waterproof?

The original Sky High is washable, not waterproof. Maybelline also sells a separate Sky High Waterproof version for swimming or humid days.

Can I wear Sky High with contact lenses?

Maybelline says the formula is ophthalmologist-tested and suitable for sensitive eyes and contact lens wearers.

How do I avoid clumps?

Start at the roots, apply thin coats one after another, and stop at about three.

How do I take it off?

Maybelline recommends micellar water or a gentle eye makeup remover. A cleansing balm or oil also works well.
