How to get the most out of it

  • Add a quality microSD card. The v4 supports cards up to 256GB, enough for days of continuous recording. The Samsung EVO Select 256GB (Pick #46) is an easy match.
  • Check your Wi-Fi band first. The camera joins 2.4 GHz networks only. If your router combines bands under one name, setup usually still works, but placing the camera within solid range of the router keeps video smooth.
  • Draw detection zones. Tap the grid in the Wyze app to ignore a busy street or swaying trees, so you get alerts for your walkway and not every passing car.
  • Use the spotlight as a deterrent. Set the spotlight (and the siren, if you like) to trigger on motion at night for an extra layer of warning at entry points.
  • Going outside? Protect the plug. The camera itself is weatherproof, but the power adapter is not; Wyze sells an outdoor power adapter, or you can run the cable to an indoor outlet through a window or soffit.

FAQ

Does the Wyze Cam v4 need a subscription?

No. Live view, motion alerts and continuous or event recording to a microSD card work without a plan. The optional Cam Plus subscription adds cloud video storage and smarter AI detection for people, packages and vehicles.

Can the Wyze Cam v4 be used outdoors?

Yes. It's rated IP65 and works from -4°F to 122°F. It does need wired power, so plan for an outlet nearby or Wyze's outdoor power adapter.

Does the Wyze Cam v4 work with 5 GHz Wi-Fi?

No. It supports 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only, using Wi-Fi 6 on that band. Most home routers broadcast 2.4 GHz alongside 5 GHz, so this rarely blocks setup.

What size microSD card does the Wyze Cam v4 take?

Wyze lists support for microSD cards up to 256GB formatted as exFAT. The card is sold separately.
