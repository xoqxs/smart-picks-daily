How to get the most out of it

  • Store the receiver inside. The little compartment next to the battery holds the USB receiver, so pop it in before you pack your bag. Tom's Hardware and Tom's Guide both highlight this as a travel saver.
  • Try Endurance mode for office use. Logitech rates Endurance mode at up to 9 months of standard use, a smart choice if the G305 is mostly your work mouse. Switch back to performance mode for gaming.
  • Match your grip to your hand. Tom's Guide switched to a claw grip and Tom's Hardware noted the low hump suits slimmer hands, so if you have medium or smaller hands, a claw or fingertip grip feels natural on this shape.
  • Dial in your DPI steps. Use G HUB to set four DPI levels you actually use, such as a low one for aiming and a higher one for desktop work. Tom's Hardware notes the button cycles through four settings, and Best Buy reviewers say it's handy mid-game.

FAQ

How long does the Logitech G305 battery last?

Logitech rates it for up to 250 hours of non-stop gaming, or up to 9 months of standard use in Endurance mode, on one AA battery. It accepts alkaline or lithium AA batteries, and one alkaline battery comes in the box.

Does the G305 work with a Mac or Chromebook?

Yes. Logitech lists support for Windows 7 or later, macOS 10.13 and later, and ChromeOS. It connects through the included USB receiver, and the G HUB software is optional.

Is the Logitech G305 good for big hands?

It's a compact mouse at 116.6 mm long, so it best suits small to medium hands. Reviewers with larger hands often prefer a claw grip on it or a bigger mouse like the Logitech G502 HERO.

Can I use the G305 without installing software?

Yes. It works as soon as you plug in the receiver. Logitech G HUB is only needed to remap buttons or change DPI levels, and the mouse saves one profile in onboard memory so settings travel with it.
