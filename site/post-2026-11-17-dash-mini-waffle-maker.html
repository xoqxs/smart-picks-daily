How to get the most out of it

  • Wait for the light. Let it fully preheat before adding batter; the first waffle comes out better that way.
  • Go easy on the batter. About 2 to 3 tablespoons is plenty. Overfilling makes thick, puffy waffles and can spill over the edges.
  • Spray once, not every time. A light coat of cooking spray at the start of a session is usually enough for several waffles.
  • Use silicone or wood tools. Skip metal spatulas and forks so the nonstick plates stay in good shape.
  • Mind the hot lid. The handle is short, so lift it by the tab and keep fingers clear of the edges; a small silicone mitt helps.
  • Make a batch for busy mornings. Cook extras, freeze them, and pop them in the toaster later.

FAQ

How big are the waffles?

Each waffle is about 4 inches across, roughly the size of a frozen toaster waffle. It cooks one at a time.

How much batter should I use?

Start with about 2 to 3 tablespoons per waffle and adjust. Too much batter makes a thicker waffle and can overflow.

Can you make chaffles in the Dash Mini?

Yes. It's a favorite for cheese-and-egg chaffles. Spread the mixture thinly and let it cook until crisp before opening.

How do you clean it?

Unplug it and let it cool completely, then wipe the plates with a damp, soapy cloth and dry. Don't submerge the unit in water.
