How to get the most out of it

  • Wipe the wand first. Tap the excess off on the rim of the tube before your first stroke; it's the single best way to prevent clumps.
  • Wiggle from the root. Start at the base of the lashes and wiggle the brush back and forth as you pull up, just as essence recommends.
  • Stop at two thin coats. Reviewers agree that coat three is where clumping starts. Let the first coat set for a moment, then add the second.
  • Use the tip for detail. Turn the wand vertically and use the narrow end for inner-corner and lower lashes.
  • Remove it gently. Micellar water or an eye makeup remover takes it off easily at night; essence pairs it with its own remover.

FAQ

Is essence Lash Princess waterproof?

The original False Lash Effect formula is not waterproof, which makes it easy to remove. essence sells a separate Lash Princess Waterproof version for humid days and events.

Is Lash Princess vegan and cruelty-free?

Yes. essence lists the mascara as vegan and PETA cruelty-free.

How do I keep Lash Princess from clumping?

Wipe excess product off the wand, apply in a wiggling motion from the roots, and stick to one or two thin coats, letting the first one set briefly.

Does it work on short lashes?

It can add length and volume, but the large brush may touch the lids. Go slowly near the lash line, or try a slimmer wand such as Maybelline Sky High.
