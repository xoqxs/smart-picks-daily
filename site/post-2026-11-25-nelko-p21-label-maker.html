How to get the most out of it

  • Calibrate before your first print. Domino found labels printed off-center until calibration was done; running it once in the app lines everything up.
  • Match the label size in the app. TechWalls recommends selecting the exact roll size before printing so text lands where you expect.
  • Stick to genuine Nelko rolls. Nelko and Windows Report both advise using P21-compatible rolls for proper feeding and print quality.
  • Use the cutter, don't tug. Press the side lever to slice the label cleanly instead of pulling it, which can jam or tear the paper.
  • Test QR codes first. Keep codes reasonably large and scan one test label before printing a whole batch, as Nelko suggests.

FAQ

Does the Nelko P21 need ink?

No. It uses direct thermal printing, so it never needs ink, toner or ribbons. You only need compatible Nelko thermal label rolls.

Can the Nelko P21 print from a computer?

The P21 is designed for mobile printing through the Nelko app on iPhone and Android. For computer printing or shipping labels, Nelko recommends its desktop or shipping label models.

What size labels does the Nelko P21 use?

It uses Nelko P21 label rolls about half an inch tall; the included roll is 14 x 40 mm, and longer 14 x 50 mm and 14 x 75 mm rolls are sold separately.

Are Nelko P21 labels waterproof?

Nelko designs P21 labels to resist water, oil and scratches for daily use. Durability varies by surface, so test one label for important items.
