How to get the most out of it

  • Start dry. Pump it onto dry hands and massage over a dry face. Water too early stops it from grabbing onto makeup.
  • Two or three pumps is plenty. Anua suggests 2 to 3 pumps; some reviewers find one is enough for a bare face.
  • Give it a full minute. Massage for about 60 seconds, paying extra attention to the nose, chin and lash line.
  • Emulsify before rinsing. Wet your fingertips and keep massaging until the oil turns milky, then rinse with lukewarm water.
  • Follow with a gentle foam or gel. A water-based cleanser finishes the double cleanse and removes any last residue.
