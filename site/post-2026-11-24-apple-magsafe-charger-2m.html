The magic of MagSafe is that you never have to line anything up. This generation is certified for Qi2 25W, so iPhone 16 and iPhone 17 models can charge noticeably faster than with older MagSafe pucks. Apple quotes up to 50% in around 30 minutes with a 30W adapter, and Macworld's test landed just over that mark. That's quick enough for a morning top-up.

FAQ

Does the Apple MagSafe charger come with a power adapter?

No. The box includes only the MagSafe Charger with its attached USB-C cable. Apple recommends a 30W USB-C power adapter for the fastest 25W charging.

Is the Apple MagSafe charger 25W?

Yes. The current model is certified for Qi2 25W and can charge compatible iPhone 16 models and newer at up to 25W with a 30W adapter. iPhone 12 through 15 models with MagSafe charge at up to 15W.

Can I charge AirPods with a MagSafe charger?

Yes. Apple lists AirPods models with a wireless or MagSafe charging case as compatible, though the puck charges one device at a time.

Does the MagSafe charger work with Android phones?

It's certified for Qi2 25W and standard Qi, and MacRumors reports it can charge Qi2 phones such as Google's Pixel 10 at up to 25W. Other Qi phones charge at lower speeds.
