FAQ

Is the Logitech Signature M650 really silent?

The left and right buttons are very quiet. Logitech claims over 90% less click noise than its M185. The side buttons and wheel click are quieter than many mice but still audible, according to reviewers and buyers.

What's the difference between the M650 and the M650 L?

Size. The M650 suits small-to-medium hands, while the M650 L is a larger body for bigger hands. Features are the same, and a left-handed M650 L Left is also available.

Does the Signature M650 work with a Mac or iPad?

Yes. It works with macOS through Bluetooth or the Logi Bolt receiver, and with iPadOS over Bluetooth. Button customization through Logi Options+ is available on Mac and Windows.

Is the Signature M650 rechargeable?

No. It uses one AA battery, which is included, and Logitech rates it for up to 24 months. Actual life depends on how much you use it.
