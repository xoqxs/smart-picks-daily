How to get the most out of it

  • Stick with Chemex filters. Tom's Guide, Reviewed and Popular Science all recommend the brand's own Bonded filters. Tom's Guide found that generic papers didn't seat well in the hourglass and let grounds slip through.
  • Face the thick side toward the spout. Seattle Coffee Gear explains that the three-layer side of the folded filter should line up with the spout so the paper doesn't block the air channel and slow the flow.
  • Grind medium-coarse. Tom's Guide and Seattle Coffee Gear both suggest a grind a little coarser than drip.
  • Rinse, then bloom. Pour hot water through the empty filter to preheat the glass, then discard it. Start each brew with a small circular pour to wet the grounds and let them bloom for about 30 seconds before adding the rest, a step Tom's Guide, Homes & Gardens and Popular Science all describe.
  • Know your fill points. Homes & Gardens notes there are no printed measuring lines. Seattle Coffee Gear says the small glass button marks about halfway and the bottom of the wood collar marks full, and a Chemex "cup" means 5 oz, not a full mug.
