How to get the most out of it

  • Wet it, then squeeze it out. Run the sponge under water until it expands (it grows noticeably), then squeeze until it's just damp, not dripping.
  • Bounce, don't wipe. Dot foundation on your face, then use quick tapping motions. Wiping drags product around; bouncing blends it in.
  • Use every side. Flat edge for big areas, rounded sides to blend edges, tip for under-eye concealer and around the nose.
  • Go dry for more coverage. If you want to cover redness or uneven tone, try a dry sponge for the first layer.
  • Clean and rotate. Rinse with lukewarm water and a gentle cleanser after use, let it air-dry fully in a ventilated spot and swap in a fresh one regularly. Real Techniques recommends replacing sponges about every 30 days, and the 4-pack makes that easy.
