How to get the most out of it

  • Pick the right shade first. Makeup artists quoted by Marie Claire and TODAY suggest going about one step lighter than your natural brows, for example a medium or soft brown instead of dark brown, for a softer result with more room for error.
  • Brush, outline, then fill. Anastasia recommends brushing hairs into place, outlining with light pressure from arch to tail, then sketching the front. Marie Claire, TotalBeauty and Yahoo describe the same outline-first approach.
  • Use a light hand and short flicks. The brand's own tip is to hold the pencil at a slant, touch it down lightly and flick in the direction your brow hairs grow. Several reviewers say that is how you get the most natural strokes.

What Brow Wiz shade should I get?

Match your natural brow hair, or go one shade lighter for a softer look. Medium Brown suits many medium-brown brows; lighter hair can look at Taupe, Blonde or Soft Brown, while dark brunettes usually lean toward Dark Brown or Ebony.

Is Brow Wiz waterproof?

Anastasia doesn't market Brow Wiz as waterproof. Reviewers still report solid all-day wear, but for swimming or heavy sweat you may want to add a brow gel on top.

What is the difference between Brow Wiz and Brow Definer?

Brow Wiz has an ultra-fine 1mm tip for detailed hair strokes and shaping. Brow Definer has a wider triangular tip that fills larger areas more quickly. Many people use the two together.

How do you use the Anastasia Brow Wiz?

Brush brows into place with the spoolie, outline lightly from arch to tail, sketch the front, add short hair-like strokes in sparse spots following the way your brows grow, and blend with the spoolie as you go.
