No smudges under the eyes

This is why many people switch to a tubing mascara in the first place. Takes off with warm water

At the end of the night, there's no need for heavy rubbing. Marie Claire's tester called taking it off "so easy," and the independent blogger simply rinses it off in the shower or presses a warm, damp cloth to her lashes until the tubes slide away.

How to get the most out of it

  • Work fast on the second coat. tarte recommends applying a second coat before the first one dries for a fuller, lash-extension look.
  • Start at the root and lift. Begin at the base of your upper lashes and sweep upward, following the natural curve out to the tips so the curl sets as it dries.
  • Curl first. Use your lash curler before you apply; reviewers say this formula keeps that curl in place once it sets.
  • Remove with warm water, not force. Wet your lashes with warm water, hold for a few seconds, then gently press with a warm, damp cloth or your fingertips. The tubes slide off instead of smearing.
  • Keep it fresh. Close the cap tightly after every use. Marie Claire notes the travel size is a handy way to finish a tube before it dries out.

FAQ

How do you remove tubing mascara?

Warm water does most of the work. Wet your lashes with warm water, give it a few seconds, then gently press with a damp cloth or your fingertips until the tubes slide off. tarte also suggests micellar water or makeup remover on a cotton pad if you prefer.

Is tarte tubing mascara waterproof?

No. It's water-resistant and sweatproof, according to tarte, but not waterproof. That's what lets it come off with warm water, so skip it for swimming.

Does tartelette tubing mascara smudge?

tarte says it's smudge-proof and flake-free for up to 24 hours, and reviewers say it holds up well with oily lids and glasses. A few people still notice some smudging, so let it dry fully before you blink hard or touch your eyes.

What's the difference between tartelette tubing and tartelette XL?

Both are tarte tubing mascaras that remove with warm water. The original is the classic for length, curl and definition, while XL is a newer formula focused on extra length and lift.
