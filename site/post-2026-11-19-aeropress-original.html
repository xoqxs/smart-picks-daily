How to get the most out of it

  • Start with a medium-fine grind. If pressing feels very hard, grind a little coarser; if the plunger sinks with almost no resistance, go finer.
  • Rinse the filter first. Pre-wetting the paper filter (and rinsing the brewer before first use) helps the filter seal and removes any papery taste.
  • Stir, wait, then press slowly. A brief stir, about a one-minute steep and a slow, gentle press gives a balanced cup.
  • Use a sturdy mug. Press onto a solid ceramic mug, not thin glassware. Many fans like brewing upside down (the "inverted" method), but AeroPress advises against it because it can tip.
  • Get the water temperature right. A temperature-control kettle such as the Chefman Glass Electric Kettle (Pick #30) makes it easy to repeat a cup you love.

FAQ

How much coffee does the AeroPress Original make?

Up to 10 oz per press, which is one to two cups or up to three espresso-style shots. You can dilute a strong concentrate with hot water to fill a larger mug.

Is the AeroPress dishwasher safe?

Yes, on the top rack. Most owners simply rinse it after popping out the grounds, which takes only a few seconds.

Does the AeroPress make real espresso?

It makes a strong, espresso-style concentrate that works well in Americanos and milk drinks, but it does not create the high pressure of an espresso machine, so you won't get true crema.

What grind should I use?

AeroPress recommends a medium-fine grind. Adjust coarser if pressing is hard, finer if the plunger drops too easily.
