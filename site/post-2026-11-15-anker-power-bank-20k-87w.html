Who it's for

Travelers, students and anyone who works from cafés with a phone and a thin laptop. If you only ever charge a phone, a smaller 10,000mAh bank will be lighter. If you need to fully recharge a 16-inch laptop, look for a higher-capacity model.

What the numbers mean in real life

Speed. Anker says the built-in cable can take an iPhone 15 Pro to 58% or a MacBook Air to 52% in 30 minutes. That's the difference between "barely enough" and "good for the afternoon" during a layover.

Capacity. In Anker's internal lab testing, a full bank gives roughly 2.98 charges for an iPhone 17 Pro, 2.39 for a Galaxy S26 Ultra and about one full charge for a MacBook Air (M2).

Sharing. 87W is the total. When a laptop and a phone charge together, the laptop gets up to 65W and the rest goes to the other devices.

A tip before you fly

Power banks belong in your carry-on, not checked luggage. Anker lists this one as approved for airline travel, but individual airlines can set their own rules, so check yours before a trip. At home, recharge it with a 65W USB-C wall charger if you want the quoted 1.5-hour refill; a slower phone charger will take much longer.
