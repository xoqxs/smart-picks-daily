How to get the most out of it

  • Give it a full first charge. BLACK+DECKER lists about four hours to recharge, and BestReviews recommends a full charge before you start. Top it up before a bigger job like a car interior.
  • Empty gently, over the trash. BestReviews notes that jostling the bowl while opening it can spill debris, so hold it low over the bin and release it slowly.
  • Wash the filter, then let it dry completely. Rinsing the filter keeps suction steady, and Just a Dad Approved suggests letting it dry overnight before putting it back.
  • Give it a home near an outlet. Pick a spot on the counter or a shelf close to a plug, and make sure the vacuum sits fully on its charger each time, a detail both BestReviews and Just a Dad Approved mention, so it's charged when the next mess happens.
  • Match the tool to the mess. Use the crevice tool for vents, seat tracks and corners, and the flip-up brush for fabric. Save deep carpet and embedded pet hair for a full-size vacuum.
