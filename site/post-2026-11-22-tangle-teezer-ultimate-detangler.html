How to get the most out of it

  • Brush while conditioner is still in. Refinery29, NewBeauty and Bustle's testimonials all suggest using it in the shower to spread conditioner, then working out knots before you rinse.
  • Work in small sections. The Quality Edit found that two or three passes per section got freshly washed hair mostly smooth. Start at the ends and move up toward the roots for the gentlest results.
  • Keep it away from heat. Tangle Teezer warns that heat can damage the teeth, so don't use it with a blow dryer or leave it next to a hot tool.
  • Clean it once in a while. Remove hair after each use, rinse in warm water with a little mild shampoo when product builds up, and let it air dry. Tangle Teezer suggests a clean toothbrush for scrubbing between the teeth.
