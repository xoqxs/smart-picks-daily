An Instant Pot 6 quart that replaces several gadgets

A calmer, safer steam release

Older Instant Pots ask you to nudge a valve by hand. The Duo Plus uses a switch that vents steam with one flick while your hand stays clear of the plume. Both reviewers liked this change, and the newest version adds a quieter, diffused release on top.

Set it, walk away, come back to dinner

Delay start lets you load ingredients in the morning and have them finish around dinnertime, and keep warm takes over automatically once cooking ends. TechRadar and Tom's Guide both point to this pairing as one of the most useful everyday features, especially for families with staggered schedules.

Cleanup is the easy part

The heavy stainless inner pot lifts out and goes into the dishwasher, and because everything happens in one vessel there's usually just a single pot and a lid to deal with. Both reviews single out the low-effort cleanup as a big reason the Duo Plus earns a permanent spot on the counter.

How to get the most out of it

  • Let the pot preheat before adding oil. Tom's Guide found that oil added too early on Sauté tends to spatter; wait for the display to show it's hot, then sear.
  • Fine-tune rice and veggie times. Tom's Guide found the default rice time a little long and suggested trimming a few minutes; jot down your favorite timings once you find them.
  • Use a steamer basket for vegetables. TechRadar's broccoli cooked unevenly under the rack; a basket keeps florets above the water for even results.
  • Handle the lid with care. The metal lid gets hot during cooking, so lift it by the handle and tilt it away from you.
  • Lean on the app. The Instant Pot app has hundreds of recipes with tested times, which takes the guesswork out of the first few weeks.

Is the Instant Pot Duo Plus dishwasher safe?

The stainless steel inner pot is dishwasher-safe. Wipe the base unit with a damp cloth and never submerge it.

Can the Instant Pot Duo Plus make yogurt?

Yes. It has a dedicated yogurt function, so you can make plain or Greek-style yogurt directly in the inner pot.
