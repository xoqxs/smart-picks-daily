Who it's for

Couples, roommates and small households who want crispy sides, reheated leftovers that aren't soggy, or a quick weeknight protein without heating a full oven. If you regularly cook two different foods at once for four or more people, a dual-zone model will suit you better.

What stands out

The dehydrate setting. Many basic air fryers start around 170°F or higher. The AF101 goes down to 105°F, which is what lets it slowly dry fruit slices or make jerky.

Reheat is its own program. It sounds minor, but leftover pizza and fries are where an air fryer beats a microwave most clearly.

Cleanup is easy. Ninja lists the removable parts as dishwasher safe, so you're not scrubbing a greasy basket by hand every night.

Tips before you buy

Don't overfill the basket. Air needs room to move, so cook fries in a single loose layer and shake halfway through. Also leave a few inches of space behind the unit on the counter, because hot air vents out the back. Finally, check the listing's color and bundle: the picture above shows an optional rack accessory, so confirm what's included in the box before ordering.

Quick checklist

  • Cooking for 1–3 people? 4 quarts is plenty.
  • Want to dehydrate? Look for a low setting like 105°F.
  • Shake the basket halfway for even crisping.
