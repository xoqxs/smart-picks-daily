Who it's for

Anyone who heat-styles regularly and wants a finishing step that also adds heat protection. It's especially popular with people who color or bleach their hair, since OLAPLEX lists it as color safe and suitable for chemically treated hair. If your hair is very fine, it can still work, but start with a single drop on the ends.

Three things worth knowing

The dispenser is the secret. You turn the bottle upside down and tap the bottom with your finger to release one drop. That small detail is why the oil is hard to overuse, and why a 1 oz bottle lasts longer than you'd expect.

It does two jobs. Use it on damp hair before blow-drying or flat-ironing for heat protection, then add a drop afterwards to smooth flyaways and boost shine.

Read the label if you avoid silicones. The first ingredient is dimethicone. That's what gives the slip and gloss, but it's not a fit for strict silicone-free or curly-girl routines.

How to use it without greasy roots

Rub the drops between your palms first, then work from the ends up to the mid-lengths. Keep it away from the scalp. If your hair feels heavy the next day, cut back to one drop instead of giving up on it.
