Why people love it

A shape your hand settles into

The sculpted body supports your palm and gives your thumb its own resting shelf, and reviewers at Tom's Hardware and TechRadar, along with many Best Buy owners, single out comfort through long sessions as the biggest win. It feels solid and premium on the desk, the kind of tool you notice only because nothing about it gets in the way.

Scrolling that feels like a superpower

The MagSpeed wheel switches between precise, line-by-line clicks and a free spin that zips through long documents in a second. Add the side thumb wheel for horizontal scrolling and wide spreadsheets or video timelines suddenly become easy to navigate. Tom's Hardware, Tom's Guide and Reddit users all rave about this combo, and many say they can't go back to a basic wheel afterward.

Clicks you can barely hear

Logitech made the 3S's buttons dramatically quieter than the previous generation while keeping a satisfying feel. TechRadar, Tom's Guide and Tom's Hardware all highlighted it, and it's a small detail that roommates, partners and coworkers will thank you for.

Buttons that work your way

With the free Logi Options+ app, you can remap the thumb buttons, the gesture button and the thumb wheel, and even set different actions for different apps. Reviewers loved assigning undo and redo, switching desktops, or turning the thumb wheel into zoom for photo and design work.

One mouse, three computers, weeks of battery

The Easy-Switch button lets you hop between up to three paired devices, which reviewers found handy for anyone juggling a work laptop and a personal computer. Battery life is rated at up to 70 days, it charges over USB-C, and you can keep using it while it's plugged in, so a low battery never stops your work.

How to get the most out of it

  • Install Logi Options+ right away. It unlocks per-app button profiles, gesture controls and Flow, which lets your cursor glide between two computers.
  • Try the thumb wheel as zoom or tab switcher. In creative apps and browsers, this tweak alone can save a surprising amount of time.
  • Use the receiver for the most stable connection. If your computer lacks Bluetooth or the connection feels sluggish, a Logi Bolt receiver usually fixes it. Note that Bolt doesn't pair with older Unifying receivers.
  • Find the Easy-Switch button underneath. Pair each computer to its own channel once, then switch with a single press.
  • Top up during a coffee break. A one-minute quick charge gives roughly three hours of use, according to Logitech.

FAQ

Does the MX Master 3S work with Mac and iPad?

Yes. Logitech lists support for macOS and iPadOS, and TechRadar used it with an iPad. Options+ customization is available on Mac and Windows.

Can it track on a glass desk?

Yes. Logitech says the Darkfield sensor works on glass at least 4 mm thick, and Tom's Hardware found it tracked accurately on glass.

Is it good for left-handed users?

No, the shape is designed for the right hand. Left-handed users will be happier with a mouse built for lefties or an ambidextrous design.

Does it come with a USB receiver?

The standard edition has traditionally included a Logi Bolt USB receiver, while the Mac and Bluetooth editions don't. Check the "in the box" details on the listing before you order.
