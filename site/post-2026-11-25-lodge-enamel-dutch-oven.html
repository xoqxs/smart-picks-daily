How to get the most out of it

  • Keep the burner at medium or below. Lodge notes that enameled cast iron holds heat so well there's no need to preheat or cook above medium, and high heat over long stretches can damage the enamel.
  • Wash by hand and skip the steel wool. Lodge recommends hand washing, and both Homes & Gardens and NBC Select warn that harsh scrubbers can scratch the coating. Soak first if anything is stuck on.
  • Keep oven mitts within reach. The loop handles get very hot on the stove and in the oven, a reminder from both CNN Underscored and NBC Select.
  • Store it with the protectors. Homes & Gardens advises storing the pot carefully so other pans don't chip the enamel, and Lodge includes lid and pot protectors for exactly that.
