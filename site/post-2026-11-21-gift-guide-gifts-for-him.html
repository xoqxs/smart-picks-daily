Gift-giving tips

  • Upgrade, don't replace his taste. A better version of something he already uses (his mouse, his travel cup, his skillet) is almost always a hit.
  • Bundle for a bigger moment. Pair the Lodge skillet with the ThermoPro thermometer for a steak-night set, or the Chemex with a bag of his favorite beans.
