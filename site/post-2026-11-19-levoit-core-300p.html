How to get the most out of it

  • Unwrap the filter first. The pre-installed filter ships sealed in plastic; take that off before you switch it on for the first time.
  • Give it some breathing room. Air comes in all the way around the base, so place it a little away from walls and curtains instead of jammed into a tight corner.
  • Run it on high, then drop to Sleep. A short blast on the top speed after cooking or cleaning freshens the room quickly; switch to Sleep Mode or the lowest speed for quiet overnight running.
  • Vacuum the outer mesh. A quick pass with a vacuum brush between filter changes keeps pet hair from clogging the intake.
  • Keep spare filters handy. Watch the check-filter light and keep a replacement on hand so you're not running it on a tired filter.

What size room is the LEVOIT Core 300-P best for?

LEVOIT lists an ideal room size of about 222 sq ft for frequent air changes, and says it can circulate the air in a 1,066 sq ft space about once an hour. It's happiest in a bedroom, home office or small living room.

Is the LEVOIT Core 300-P loud?

On its lowest setting and in Sleep Mode it's very quiet, starting at a rated 24 dB. The top speed is noticeably louder, so most people use it for a quick refresh and then turn it down.

Does it have Wi-Fi or an app?

No. The Core 300-P uses simple touch controls on the top panel. If you want app control and scheduling, choose the Core 300S.

How do I change the filter?

Turn the purifier over, twist the bottom cover to open it, pull out the old cylinder filter, drop in the new one and reset the check-filter indicator. It takes about a minute.
