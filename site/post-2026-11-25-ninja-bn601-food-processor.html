How to get the most out of it

  • Cut big foods to fit the chute. TechGearLab and ShopSavvy both mention the feed chute is on the narrow side, so halve large potatoes, tomatoes or cabbage wedges before you start and the disc will run nonstop.
  • Start with smaller batches. BestReviews suggests beginning with small amounts to learn how quickly it works, and Homes & Gardens recommends halving large recipes if you're unsure they'll fit. It's the easiest way to avoid turning chopped onions into puree.
  • Let go of Pulse a beat early. Both TechGearLab and BestReviews noticed the blade keeps spinning briefly after you release the button, so stop just before you reach the texture you want.
  • Handle the blades with care and rinse right away. The stacked quad blade is very sharp, as BestReviews and TechGearLab point out. Rinse it soon after use so food doesn't dry on, then place it on the top rack of the dishwasher, removing it from the bowl first as Ninja advises.
