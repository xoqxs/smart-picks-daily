How to get the most out of it

  • Preheat while you prep. Start the four-minute preheat as you season your food, so the basket is hot the moment you're ready. Reviewers say it pays off most with fries and frozen snacks.
  • Shake or flip at the halfway mark. There's no built-in reminder, so a quick phone timer keeps browning even on every piece.
  • Don't crowd the basket. A single layer with a little breathing room crisps far better than a heaped pile. Cook big batches in two rounds.
  • Use just a splash of oil. Food browns well with little or no oil, but a light toss (a tablespoon or two at most) gives potatoes and veggies extra crunch.
  • Check thin foods early. Sweet potatoes, thin fish fillets and nuggets often finish ahead of the package time, so peek a few minutes before the timer ends.
